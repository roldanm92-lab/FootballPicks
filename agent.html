Agent Play Card

How far to trust this

The agent was replayed on every NFL prop this season, using only games played before each one, with each price carrying the books' real hold (about 7%). A play shows here only if it passes a setting that beat the price in that replay. Results swung week to week, so treat this as a shortlist, not a lock.

Prices: unders show the books' consensus under price and the best book where props.cash lists both sides. When it doesn't, the under price is estimated from the over and the play is capped at Lean; check it at your book. Re-check injuries close to game time.
