Agent Play Card

How far to trust this

Every time this page is built, the agent is replayed on every prop this season, using only games played before each one, with each price carrying the books' real hold (about 7%). Strong and Lean plays come only from play types that are making money in that replay right now. Play types that are losing are shown as Watch, so you can follow them without betting them.

Chances lane (NBA rebounds): rebound overs where the player's last-10 rebound chances (props.cash tracking) are at least 2.4 times the line. Backtest: 95-56, +16.5%. Rebound overs from hit rates alone lost money, so rebounds only come from this lane.

Form lane (NBA): overs where the player's own hit rate (last 10 + season) beats the price's no-vig chance by 25+ points, his last-20 hit rate also beats it by 10+, and he hit in at least 3 of his last 5. It skips bench players in likely blowouts, points overs in low-total games, spots where a same-position starter is out (the line has usually already moved), and prices longer than +200 (often mis-scrapes). Both lanes together, backtested on every pre-game snapshot of this site: 264-175, +14.0% over 439 bets, positive in both halves and on 16 of 19 days, so these show as Lean. Every other props.cash field was tested too (defense ranks and splits, matchup rating, projection, head-to-head, minutes, shot zones, play types, team form, back-to-backs and rest, home/road splits, every book's price); none added a reliable edge, so they appear on each card as context only. Hot streaks without last-20 support lost 25% and are left out.

Prices: unders show the books' consensus under price and the best book where props.cash lists both sides. When it doesn't, the under price is estimated from the over and the play is capped at Lean; check it at your book. Re-check injuries close to game time.
